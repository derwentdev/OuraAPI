# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 24-JUL-26

This application ("the App") is a personal project that connects to the Oura API to retrieve health and activity data (such as sleep, readiness, and activity metrics) on behalf of the authorized user.

## What data we access

With your authorization, the App accesses data made available through the Oura API, which may include sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, and related wellness data associated with your Oura account.

## How data is used

Data retrieved from the Oura API is used solely for the personal, non-commercial purposes of the App's operator. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

## How data is stored

Data is stored only as needed to operate the App (for example, locally on the operator's device or in a private database/environment variable store). It is not made publicly accessible.

## Data retention and deletion

You may revoke the App's access to your Oura data at any time through your Oura account settings. Upon revocation, the App will no longer be able to retrieve new data. To request deletion of previously retrieved data, contact matthew.nermoe@gmail.com.
